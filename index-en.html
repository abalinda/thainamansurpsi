Thainá Mansur

Psychologist - Psychoanalyst

With a degree in Psychology from the University of Brasília (UnB), I dedicate myself to clinical practice through the psychoanalytic approach. My practice is grounded in analytic listening, providing a welcoming space for the working through of psychic suffering.

I offer sessions for adolescents and adults, working on issues such as anxiety, depression, relationships, compulsions, phobias, and personality disorders.

  • Psychoanalytic Approach

    Treatment based on analytic listening, allowing for the understanding of psychic suffering and its deeper meanings.

  • Brazilians Abroad

    Specialized in attending Brazilians living abroad, offering a welcoming space to deal with the emotional challenges of life outside the country.

  • Online Sessions

    Remote sessions available through a secure platform, ensuring your treatment continues even from a distance.

  • Who I Work With

    Sessions for adolescents and adults, with experience in various emotional demands.

  • Academic Background

    Graduated from University of Brasilia (UnB) with an ongoing specialization in Psychoanalysis, maintaining continuous education.

  • Cost

    Fees are set in accordance with the Brazilian Federal Council of Psychology standards for individual therapy
